ECE-CTE Senior Assignment

Goal Setting: ECE Reflections & CLASS Feedback

Instructions: You have reflected on your strengths, and your instructor has given you feedback based on the CLASS tool above. Now, turn those reflections into two specific goals for your teaching practice.

1 Part 1: Building on Your Strengths

Look at your recent reflection about what energizes you and a past success. Then, look at the feedback your teacher gave you using CLASS terms (like Teacher Sensitivity, Quality of Feedback, or Regard for Student Perspectives).

Example: Because I enjoy talking with the kids, and my teacher noted my "Regard for Student Perspectives," my goal is to ask two open-ended questions to let children lead the conversation during center time.

2 Part 2: Area for Growth

Look at the reflection you wrote about a specific thing you want to do differently next time you are in the early childhood classroom, alongside any Low/Mid CLASS indicators above.

Example: Next time, I want to handle transitions better. My goal is to use a 2-minute warning song before cleanup time so children know what to expect.
